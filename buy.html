Cohorts: ranges, and ten benchmarks

T-CARS-1, two rungs. Where two or more certified results share a model and a currency, the range they span, computed only from the certified records above, with the count shown: a range is context, not a benchmark. Where a cohort reaches eight certified results in one currency, the trigger priced in BENCHMARK.md, the cohort also prints its median, computed in the build from the certified records alone and published in the dataset. Ten cohorts have met the trigger: the Ferrari F40 in euros at stage 7, the Ford GT in dollars at stage 8, the Corvette and the Duesenberg Model J in dollars at stage 9, the Aston Martin DB5 in dollars, and the Lamborghini Miura in dollars and in euros at stage 10, the Ferrari F40 in dollars and the Aston Martin DB5 in euros at stage 11, and the McLaren F1 in dollars at stage 12. Every cohort of two or more is now a benchmark cohort; no range-only cohort remains. A median of past sales is a benchmark of what examples have done, not a valuation of any other example, and no condition tier is claimed. See BENCHMARK.md for the priced road from here.

Price Watch, designed in the open

The results above are real: completed auction results, certified one by one against a published source. The later stages, listing ingest adapters per channel, normalized to one schema; price history per listing and per model-year cohort; drop detection against cohort medians; opt-in alerts, ship only when the data behind them is real. Until a stage is real, it doesn't appear on this site. Every record carries its date; a stale price will be labeled stale, never dressed up as live.
