How this grows

The registry mixes national clubs and major networks with the first chapter-level entries (wave 2, regional chapters with their own calendars, starting in Northern California). Wave 3 added marque clubs for the historic batch, Duesenberg (via the ACD Club), Packard, Saab, Studebaker, DeLorean, Pontiac and Oldsmobile. Later waves add more chapters with cities, meeting cadence, and event calendars, so "near you" gets literal. Details were accurate as seeded; confirm current terms on each club's own site.
