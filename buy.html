Completed results, not live listings. Each was certified against a published source naming the car, the price, the house, and the date, and the source is linked on the record.

Following, in this browser

Models you follow from their record pages, with their newest certified result first. Following is stored in this browser only; it notifies nobody, and learning what is new means looking here.

Cohorts: ranges, and thirteen benchmarks

Where two or more certified results share a model and a currency, the range they span prints below, with the count: a range is context, not a valuation. At eight certified results in one currency a cohort also prints its median, computed from the certified records alone: a benchmark of what examples have done, not a valuation of any other example, and no condition tier is claimed. Thirteen cohorts print benchmarks; two, the Ferrari Enzo in euros and in dollars, print ranges only, six sales short of the trigger. The stage-by-stage history is in BENCHMARK.md and the WAVELOG.

Price Watch, designed in the open

The results above are real: completed auction results, certified one by one against a published source. Later stages (listing ingest, price history, drop detection, opt-in alerts) ship only when the data behind them is real; until a stage is real, it doesn't appear on this site. Every record carries its date; a stale price is labeled stale, never dressed up as live.
